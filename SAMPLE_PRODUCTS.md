## 📝 How to Add These Products

1. Go to `/admin/products/new`
2. Copy product details from above
3. Search for official product images on:
   - Manufacturer website
   - Google Images (filter: "larger than 1024x768")
   - Unsplash (royalty-free)
   - Pexels (royalty-free)
4. Download 3-5 high-quality images per product
5. Drag & drop images in admin panel
6. Fill in all fields
7. Click "Create Product"

## 💡 Product Photography Tips (Like ido.lk)

1. **White Background** for main image (clean, professional)
2. **Product in Use** for secondary images (lifestyle shots)
3. **Close-ups** of key features (ports, buttons, materials)
4. **Packaging** shots (unboxing appeal)
5. **Size Comparison** images (show scale)
