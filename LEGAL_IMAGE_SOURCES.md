# 📸 Legal Image Sources for Your Store

## ✅ FREE & LEGAL Image Sources

### 1. **Unsplash.com** (FREE - Commercial Use)
```
https://unsplash.com/s/photos/smartphone
https://unsplash.com/s/photos/laptop
https://unsplash.com/s/photos/headphones
https://unsplash.com/s/photos/smartwatch
https://unsplash.com/s/photos/camera
```
- High quality professional photos
- No attribution required
- Commercial use allowed
- Download original size

### 2. **Pexels.com** (FREE - Commercial Use)
```
https://pexels.com
```
- Free stock photos and videos
- No attribution required
- Commercial use allowed
- High quality images

### 3. **Pixabay.com** (FREE - Commercial Use)
```
https://pixabay.com
```
- Over 2 million free images
- Commercial use allowed
- No attribution required

### 4. **Manufacturer Websites** (LEGAL - With Permission)

**For Electronics:**
- **Apple:** apple.com/newsroom (press images)
- **Samsung:** news.samsung.com/global (media library)
- **Sony:** sony.net/brand (brand resources)
- **Xiaomi:** mi.com/global (product images)
- **ASUS:** asus.com (product pages - screenshot allowed)

**How to Use:**
1. Visit product page
2. Right-click → Save image
3. Or take screenshots
4. Credit manufacturer in description

### 5. **Product APIs** (LEGAL - Free Tiers)

**Amazon Product Advertising API:**
- Get product images legally
- Requires affiliate account
- Free to use
- High quality images

**eBay API:**
- Access product images
- Free developer account
- Commercial use allowed

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

### Illegal Actions:
❌ Scraping images from ido.lk or competitors
❌ Using copyrighted product photos without permission
❌ Copying product descriptions word-for-word
❌ Using brand logos without authorization
❌ Hotlinking images from other sites

### Why It's Wrong:
- Copyright infringement
- Legal liability
- Website can ban your IP
- Damages your reputation
- Can face lawsuits

## 🎨 Create Professional Product Photos

### Option 1: Take Your Own Photos
- Use smartphone with good camera
- White background (use white paper/cloth)
- Good lighting (natural or LED)
- Multiple angles
- Close-ups of features

2. Edit in Canva (free)
3. Add your branding
4. Consistent style across products

### Option 3: Hire Photographer
- Local photographers
- Fiverr.com
- Upwork.com
- One-time cost
- Own the images forever

## 🛡️ Protect Yourself Legally

### Image Rights:
1. Always check license before using
2. Keep records of where images came from
3. Give attribution if required
4. Use royalty-free images
5. Never use watermarked images

### Text Content:
1. Write original descriptions
2. Use manufacturer specs (public info)
3. Add your unique selling points
4. Don't copy competitors word-for-word
5. Use your own voice
